# Quant Desk Wins by Proving, Not Predicting

Quant Desk already beats MetaTrader 4/5, NinjaTrader, Trade Ideas and TrendSpider at the one thing none of them sells credibly: evidence. It trails all four on the live trading terminal: streaming prices, intraday charts, drawing tools, user-built scans, alerts, watchlists and order execution. None of the four is a real NSE competitor. The RBI's Alert List names MT4 and MT5 as unauthorised forex platforms, FEMA rules stop Indian residents funding NinjaTrader futures accounts, and Trade Ideas and TrendSpider carry no NSE stocks. The practical rivals are therefore TradingView, Chartink, Streak, Sensibull, AlgoTest and FYERS's own free tools. Together they cost an active trader roughly ₹2,500–6,000 a month, and they still leave survivorship-free backtests with realistic costs and tax, and verified signal records, unserved. Those are exactly Quant Desk's strengths. The owner's two complaints come from design, not bugs. First, prices reach the browser only through a 30-second REST poll that runs 09:15–15:30 and feeds three widgets; without a FYERS login it falls back to Yahoo data about 15 minutes old. Second, there is no per-stock verdict at all: only model-portfolio orders, an options gate whose designed default is NO TRADE, and 18 overlapping label vocabularies. Most of those NO TRADEs are truthful. None of 23 tested candle patterns and none of 12 intraday strategies shows an edge after costs, and all 6 journalled options verdicts were NO TRADE. So the cure is not looser gates. It is a FYERS WebSocket stream pushed to the browser, plus one per-symbol Action card (BUY, SELL-to-exit, HOLD, NO EDGE) driven only by working strategies and the owner's holdings. The "not investment advice" label marks a legal line. Personal research is allowed, and so is automating one's own FYERS account (static IP, daily two-factor login, at most 10 orders a second). Doing the same for other people is regulated: giving signals for any direct or indirect benefit requires SEBI Research Analyst registration, executing for them requires an exchange-empanelled provider hosted by the broker, and showing them live NSE prices requires an NSE data licence. The recommended plan runs in four phases: trust fixes in two weeks, live data and the Action layer, research depth and user-driven discovery, then compliant own-account execution. Going multi-user is a separate legal decision.

It adds multi-symbol testing, genetic optimisation, a forward-test split, visual replay and distributed runs ([MT5 tick generation](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/tick_generation); [MT5 Strategy Tester](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/automated-trading/strategy-tester)). The MQL5 Cloud Network showed **65,630 agents online and 21.09 billion tasks processed** in November 2026 ([cloud.mql5.com](https://cloud.mql5.com/en/stats)).

MetaQuotes shipped roughly one build every three to four weeks through 2025–2026. Those builds added ONNX inference on CUDA, .NET import and a Python package update. Build 6060 (23 July 2026) added native MCP and agentic AI, which can now launch optimisations and read tester logs ([MT5 release notes](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes)). MT5 carried **65% of MetaQuotes-platform retail CFD volume in Q1 2026** ([Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/north-america-holds-the-line-for-mt4-as-mt5-sweeps-five-of-six-regions/)).

Its weaknesses are structural: only three native chart types; web and mobile terminals that cannot run Expert Advisors or custom indicators ([IG](https://www.ig.com/en/help-and-support/platforms/mt5/can-i-use-mt5-on-mac)); a Mac build that is the Windows app inside a Wine wrapper ([MQL5 forum](https://www.mql5.com/en/forum/508861)); reviewers who say the charts "look like they were drawn by hand" and that the mobile app is slow ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/metatrader5.com)); and an MQL5 Market where buyers describe EAs that pass backtests and then "blow the accounts", with refunds refused ([Trustpilot: mql5.com](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.mql5.com)). The terminal itself is free; traders pay through broker spreads, a VPS at about USD 10–15 a month, Market products at USD 30–1,699, and the 20% commission that the Signals copy-trading service keeps ([MQL5 Market](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/mt5); [Signals rules](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/rules)).

**NinjaTrader is the benchmark for futures order flow and trade management.** Kraken completed its $1.5 billion acquisition on 1 May 2025 ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250501627804/en/Kraken-Completes-Acquisition-of-NinjaTrader)). NinjaTrader offers every non-time-based bar type the owner listed, plus three order-flow bar types ([NT8 Help: Bar Types](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/bar_types.htm)). Its eight-tool Order Flow+ suite includes footprint "volumetric" bars, a depth heatmap and cumulative delta ([NT8 Help: Order Flow+](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/order_flow_plus.htm)). Trade management centres on SuperDOM ladder entry and ATM brackets that attach stops and targets to every entry ([NinjaTrader platform](https://ninjatrader.com/trading-platform/)).

On 10 November 2026 it shipped an **AI Strategy Builder** that turns plain English into compiled NinjaScript and backtests it automatically ([AI Strategy Builder](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/ai-strategy-builder-beta.htm)). Its beta MCP server exposes `place_order`, `modify_order` and `close_position` to Claude and ChatGPT ([NinjaTrader MCP](https://docs.ninjatrader.com/mcp)). Live plans cost $0, $99 a month or $1,499 lifetime, with per-side commissions falling from $1.29 to $0.09 ([NinjaTrader pricing](https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/)).

Its weaknesses are a Windows-only desktop with a steep learning curve, in a 4.0/5 independent rating ([StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/futures/review/ninjatrader)); recurring complaints of crashes and connection drops; and slow backtests. Staff said a backtest runs on one logical processor, and one user reports **about four hours to run one month of tick replay** ([NinjaTrader forum](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/1268802-slow-backtest-optimisation-with-tick-replay)). BrokerChooser also relays a $250,000 NFA fine (May 2025) and a CFTC order of over $980,000 (September 2024), which I have not verified against the regulators ([BrokerChooser](https://www.brokerchooser.com/broker-reviews/ninjatrader-review)).

**Trade Ideas is the benchmark for real-time scanning and for packaging AI as clear calls.** It offers 310 streaming alerts and 249 filters. Alerts fire on every qualifying trade, and Top Lists refresh every 15 seconds ([Trade Ideas speed page](https://www.trade-ideas.com/how-fast-is-trade-ideas/)). Holly backtests "over 60" proprietary, non-customisable intraday strategies each night and deploys the best the next day. That typically yields 5–25 calls a session, each with an entry and a stop usually about 3% away ([Holly guide](https://www.trade-ideas.com/hollyguide/print.html); [Trade Ideas homepage](https://trade-ideas.com/)). Trustpilot shows **3.9/5 from 51 reviews, 35% of them one-star**, with complaints that Holly is "very weak" and "Not accurate" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trade-ideas.com)).

**TrendSpider is the benchmark for automated chart reading and AI agents, and it is unusually candid about its own limits.** It draws trendlines, Fibonacci levels and S/R heatmaps automatically and recognises 100+ candlestick patterns. But it recognises **only 13 chart patterns, which cannot be alerted on or backtested** and can vanish when the chart reloads ([TrendSpider chart patterns](https://help.trendspider.com/kb/automated-technical-analysis/automated-chart-pattern-recognition)). Scans are capped at 3,000 symbols and "do not auto-refresh" ([scanner FAQ](https://help.trendspider.com/kb/scanner/market-scanner-faq-markdown-html)). The Strategy Tester assumes "perfect execution and zero slippage" and that "you can always afford an entry" ([Strategy Tester help](https://help.trendspider.com/kb/strategy-tester/understanding-strategy-tester-from-trendspider)).

Every plan includes only 25 Sidekick messages a month; one reviewer used 18 "in a couple of hours" ([StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/trendspider)). Live trading needs a separate SignalStack subscription, whose 32 brokers include none from India ([SignalStack](https://signalstack.com/pricing/)). By the research notes' arithmetic, an Enhanced plan plus Sidekick Plus, SignalStack Premium and options data costs **roughly $360–420 a month**. Its strengths are support (Trustpilot 4.8 from 942 reviews) and monthly releases ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendspider.com); [October 2026 changelog](https://trendspider.com/blog/october-2026-changelog/)).

Together the four define what was table stakes in late 2026: natural-language scan and strategy building, an AI agent wired into the platform's own tools, Monte Carlo and walk-forward validation, and tick-driven alerts. They also share three weaknesses that Quant Desk can exploit: power features tied to Windows, opaque performance marketing, and no Indian market coverage.

## Performance claims range from unpublished to cherry-picked

**Trade Ideas is the starkest case.** Its current "Holly Records" page measures "profit potential" from the signal price to that session's high or low. It shows only "selected" examples, every one a winner, headlined by **BNAI +2,905% on 27 January 2026** ([Holly Records](https://tradeideas.lpcontent.net/holly-records)).

The only full-distribution figure with a stated method dates from August 2018. A simulated $1 million portfolio returned +54% in the first half of 2018 against SPY's +3%. It made 1,811 trades with a **44.9% win rate and a 1.77 profit factor**, slippage "not factored in", and no audit ([Trade Ideas, Aug 2018](https://www.trade-ideas.com/2018/08/03/our-a-i-managed-simulated-portfolio-smashes-market-benchmarks-for-1st-half-of-2018/)). That sub-50% win rate contradicts the "60% success rate" users still quote back in complaints ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trade-ideas.com)).

Later figures are no better sourced. The 2025 numbers attributed to Holly (about a 62% win rate and 25% a year) come from a competitor that sells a rival product. They assume fills at the signal price and no commissions ([TradeAlgo](https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/ai-trading/trade-ideas-review)). The one mainstream independent reviewer says Holly's results "appear cherry-picked" ([StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/trade-ideas)).

**TrendSpider publishes no accuracy figures for its pattern or trendline detection.** The "34 of 40 setups" figure in affiliate reviews has no stated method ([GreatWorkLife](https://www.greatworklife.com/trendspider-review/)). ML Quant Lab launched with no quantified performance claims ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/trendspider-launches-ai-strategy-lab-to-empower-investors-to-build-custom-ai-powered-trading-models-with-ease-302306657.html)). An independent reviewer found that "most strategies the reviewer built were flagged as unacceptable in the lab" ([StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/tools/trendspider)).

To its credit, TrendSpider's help page warns that test-set results are biased when optimisation picks the model, and recommends true out-of-sample tests ([TrendSpider help](https://help.trendspider.com/kb/ai-strategies/creating-an-ai-strategy-5-assessing-quality-of-models)). Its other claims are unverified vendor numbers: "~400 strategies per 5 minutes", plus SignalStack's sub-0.45-second routing and "99.99%" uptime ([October 2026 changelog](https://trendspider.com/blog/october-2026-changelog/); [SignalStack](https://signalstack.com/pricing/)).

**MetaQuotes and NinjaTrader publish adoption, not performance.** MT5's tester page promises results in "a few minutes" instead of "days, weeks or months", with no throughput figure ([MT5 Strategy Tester](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/automated-trading/strategy-tester)). One MQL5 Signals listing advertises "2 331% growth since 2024" and 99% reliability for USD 35 a month. A statistic like that is easy to manufacture over a short track record ([MQL5 Signals](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/mt5)).

NinjaTrader publishes no latency or backtest-speed figures, and its user counts conflict. It claimed "nearly 2 million traders" in March 2025 and "almost 4.5 million" in October 2026, while its ecosystem page says "over 800,000 active users" ([NinjaTrader press release](https://ninjatrader.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-ninjatrader-introducing-the-next-era-of-professional-trading/); [UK launch](https://ninjatrader.com/news/ninjatrader-launches-in-the-uk-to-meet-rising-demand-for-futures-trading/); [customize page](https://www.ninjatrader.com/trading-platform/customize/)).

**India's signal market is worse.** Liquide advertises a "70% Hit Rate… Checked by a Chartered Accountant" without publishing the method ([Liquide](https://liquide.life/landing/btst-1)). Multyfi claims "over 80% accuracy" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ly/app/multyfi-app/id6748902495)). SEBI's own studies point the other way. **91% of individual equity F&O traders lost money in FY25**, with net losses of about ₹1.06 lakh crore ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html)). In FY26, 87.7% were still loss-making ([Finnovate](https://www.finnovate.in/learn/blog/sebi-fno-trader-losses-fy26-individual-derivatives)). SEBI's PaRRVA verification route became operational in May 2026, and after May 2028 only PaRRVA-verified metrics may be communicated ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-parrva-framework-performance-verification-deadlines-126042901492_1.html)).

Against that backdrop, Quant Desk's existing habits are a market gap rather than a quirk. Claude forecasts are journalled, scored against a coin flip and labelled "unproven" until 60 have settled (`scanner/ai_forecast.py:1-14, 316-317`); the pattern study reports that none of 23 patterns has an edge (`data/results/pattern_study.json`); and every journalled BUY has a live track record (`scanner/track.py:1-14`). No competitor reviewed publishes anything comparable. This transparency costs Quant Desk little to keep and is costly for rivals to copy, because it would expose their marketing.

## India's retail toolchain is fragmented and newly paywalled

**TradingView became a paid product for live NSE data in September 2026.** Around 18–20 September, free Indian accounts started seeing NSE prices delayed by 15 minutes ([AlgoTest](https://algotest.in/blog/tradingview-charts-showing-delayed-data/); [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/why-is-no-one-talking-about-this-tradingview-data-delay/198284)). Real-time NSE now needs the Essential plan at ₹1,295 a month, or ₹995 a month billed annually. The free plan allows 2 indicators per chart and 3 price alerts; Ultimate allows 1,000 alerts ([OneTradeJournal](https://onetradejournal.com/platforms/tradingview-plans-india)). FYERS, Dhan, Angel One, Upstox and others let clients trade from TradingView charts; Zerodha does not ([OneTradeJournal](https://onetradejournal.com/platforms/brokers-connected-to-tradingview); [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/zerodhas-integration-with-tradingview/177136)). The webhook route that hobbyist automation relied on is closed for FYERS clients. FYERS API Bridge stopped selling subscriptions on 5 March 2026 and stopped placing orders from 1 April 2026 ([FYERS notice](https://fyers.in/notice-board/api-bridge-subscription-discontinuation-order-placement-changes/)).

**Scanning is split by data type and by broker.** Chartink sells real-time scans, 1–3-minute timeframes and webhook alerts for ₹780 per 30 days or ₹8,500 a year; its free tier is delayed about 5 minutes, and it has no app and no refunds ([Tapetide](https://tapetide.com/compare/stock-research-subscription-prices-india); [Strike](https://www.strike.money/reviews/chartink)). Streak has been free to Zerodha clients since 17 January 2024, with 250+ scanners and 150+ strategies, but it allows only 5 live deployments and 5 conditions per rule, with no multi-timeframe logic, no multi-leg options and no broker other than Zerodha ([Zerodha](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/?p=368853); [Intraday Lab](https://intradaylab.com/blog/algo-trading-without-coding-india-platforms-comparison)). Screener.in (₹4,999 a year) dominates fundamentals, and Trendlyne sells DVM scores and screener backtests capped at 200–1,200 a year ([Tapetide](https://tapetide.com/compare/stock-research-subscription-prices-india)).

**Options analytics is commoditised at the live end and thin at the historical end.** Sensibull is free for Zerodha users and about ₹800–1,300 a month otherwise; it has no historical backtesting, and users complain that predicted P&L differs from actual ([Strike](https://www.strike.money/reviews/sensibull); [OneTradeJournal](https://onetradejournal.com/platforms/options-analytics-tools-compared)). Opstra costs ₹1,300 a month with end-of-day backtests, and Quantsapp ₹4,000–8,000 a month. AlgoTest Starter costs ₹499 a month, with 25 free backtests a week; it runs multi-leg options backtests that model slippage, brokerage and STT, including the post-Budget 2026 rates, connects to 20+ brokers and claims NSE/BSE empanelment as an algo vendor ([AlgoTest](https://algotest.in/blog/algo-trading-software-price-in-india/); [Intraday Lab](https://intradaylab.com/blog/algo-trading-without-coding-india-platforms-comparison)). Tradetron still carries the overhang of SEBI's October 2024 scrutiny of more than 120 brokers over marketplace strategies that implied assured returns ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-scrutinises-brokers-linked-to-algo-trading-with-guaranteed-returns-124100901218_1.html)).

FYERS, the owner's broker, already gives clients a free API and TradingView-based charts. It also offers a Strategy Builder with payoff and Greeks, screeners through the API, and FYERS Automate for no-code automation (5 automations on Standard, unlimited on Prime) ([FYERS algo trading](https://fyers.in/offerings/algo-trading); [FYERS recap April 2026](https://fyers.in/community/t/fyers-product-recap-april-2026/12280)).

An active trader's stack of TradingView Essential, Chartink, Sensibull or Opstra, AlgoTest and Kite Connect costs roughly ₹2,500–6,000 a month, yet it still leaves six gaps: point-in-time, survivorship-free universes, which no retail tool reviewed advertises; a dated Indian cost engine; unified equity and F&O backtesting; honest strategy statistics; broker-agnostic, compliant execution; and cheaper real-time charting. The cost engine matters because costs keep changing: STT on options premium rose from 0.0625% to 0.1% and then to 0.15% from 1 April 2026, and futures STT is now 0.05% ([Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/explainer-zerodha-ups-trading-fee-others-may-follow-discount-brokers-going-eternal-way-522486-2026-03-26); [Strike](https://www.strike.money/?p=5233)). Quant Desk already fills the first gap with each day's 500 most-traded NSE stocks since August 2018, including 74 delisted names (`scanner/pit.py`; `README.md:84-90`). It largely fills the fourth with evidence grades, a timing range, after-tax returns and factor-index comparison (`README.md:91-116`), and partly fills the second with FYERS costs plus 20.8% STCG and 13% LTCG (`scanner/costs.py:1-14`; `config.yaml`). **Quant Desk's moat sits exactly where Indian vendors leave the market open, and its holes sit where FYERS and TradingView already give good tools away.**

## Prices freeze because three widgets poll a REST endpoint

**The freeze is built into the architecture.** The front-end hooks file states the rule plainly: "Scan-derived data never goes stale on a timer… Only /api/status polls" (`web/ui/src/api/hooks.ts:1-2`). The global query defaults are `staleTime: Infinity` and `refetchOnWindowFocus: false` (`web/ui/src/main.tsx:14-22`). `useLiveQuotes` is enabled only while the session clock reads "open", from 09:15 to 15:30 on trading days. It refetches every 30 seconds and returns an empty map at all other times (`web/ui/src/api/hooks.ts:89-101`; `web/api/services/clock.py:49-56`).

Those live quotes reach only three places: the top-bar index ticker, which is hidden below 1,024 px (`web/ui/src/components/layout/TopBar.tsx:105`); the Chart page's live pill and last candle, on the daily timeframe only (`web/ui/src/components/charts/PriceChart.tsx:207-224`); and the Signals page's "Now ₹" column. A few specialist panels poll their own endpoints every 15–180 seconds: the option chain, F&O "Live today", the big-money tape and levels. Everything else is a scan snapshot, including the Overview tiles, Screener, Portfolio P&L, Market movers, strategy pages and the price in the chart's side panel. A snapshot changes only when a new scan lands, at best every 30 minutes between 09:45 and 15:15, and only if the Windows Task Scheduler jobs are registered and the owner is signed in (`web/api/services/automation_svc.py:25-36`; `README.md:359-365`).

**The data source is often stale too.** The server uses FYERS REST quotes only if the owner logged in today and more than 600 FYERS data calls remain. Otherwise, or after any auth or rate-limit error, it falls back to a Yahoo daily-interval download that is about 15 minutes late. Per-symbol caches last 30 seconds for FYERS and 60 seconds for Yahoo (`web/api/services/quotes_svc.py:23-25, 89-108`; `scanner/providers.py:222-239`). So a value on screen can be **15+ minutes old and change only once a minute**. Even with FYERS, the screen gets a REST snapshot every 30–60 seconds, not a tick stream.

Three behaviours make the platform look broken rather than merely slow. The Chart toolbar shows a live price while the side panel beside it shows the scan price (`web/ui/src/pages/Chart.tsx:112, 361`); at 15:30 the live map empties, so numbers jump back to the 15:15 scan until the 15:40 scan arrives (`web/ui/src/components/layout/TopBar.tsx:75-76`); and nothing refetches when the owner returns to the tab. None of this is tested: the frontend has six vitest files for `src/lib` helpers and no component or end-to-end tests (`web/ui/package.json`).

**FYERS limits explain why faster polling is not the answer.** On the Standard plan, Data APIs allow **5 calls a second, 50 a minute and 5,000 a day**, with 50 symbols per quote request. Exceeding the per-minute limit more than three times in a day blocks the user for the rest of that day ([FYERS Plans & Limits](https://fyers.in/api-docs/v3/getting-started/plans-and-limits/)).

Polling the Nifty 500 every 30 seconds takes 10 requests per poll and 750 polls across a 375-minute session. That is **7,500 calls a day, 50% over the Standard cap**, before any scanning or history calls. The WebSocket has no such budget. It streams **100 symbols on Standard and 5,000 on Prime**. Prime costs ₹499 a month plus GST and raises the Data API allowance to 500,000 calls a day ([FYERS WebSocket](https://fyers.in/api-docs/v3/api/websockets/websocket-overview/); [FYERS Prime](https://fyers.in/prime)).

One more constraint shapes the design. FYERS allows **only one Data socket instance per user at a time**, and Quant Desk's background live service already holds it, for up to 100 symbols, to build 5-minute bars and footprints (`scanner/intraday/live.py:1-4, 179, 228`). The browser stream must therefore be relayed from that socket, not opened as a second one.

**The fix has three layers.**

**Transport.** The live service becomes a quote hub that publishes ticks to a local channel. A FastAPI Server-Sent Events endpoint relays them to a browser `EventSource`. The browser subscribes only to the symbols visible on the current page, plus holdings and watchlists. The client patches TanStack Query caches directly instead of refetching, and coalesces updates to a few renders per symbol per second.

**Presentation.** Every price on every page gets the live overlay, including the side panel, Portfolio P&L, Overview tiles, Paper mark-to-market and a "last price" column in the Screener. Screener rankings stay scan-based and are labelled as such. Every number carries a stamp, such as "as of 10:42:17 · FYERS live", "Yahoo · delayed 15 min" or "Close 11 Nov". Delayed data gets a red badge, and a live tick gets a staleness colour once it is more than a few seconds old. At 15:30, prices should switch to "last trade, market closed" instead of emptying, until the end-of-day scan lands. Scan data should refetch when the tab regains focus.

**Operations.** FYERS tokens expire at 06:30 each day and need fresh two-factor login ([FYERS authentication](https://fyers.in/api-docs/v3/api/authentication/)). A Telegram nudge around 08:45 and a prominent "log in for live prices" banner therefore matter as much as the code. The scheduler should move to an always-on machine. Order placement will need a static IP there anyway, and the jobs currently stop whenever Windows is signed out.

The same stream, with a FYERS history backfill, also delivers the 1-minute and 5-minute chart that the disabled 5m button is waiting for (`web/ui/src/pages/Chart.tsx:321`). A Playwright test that drives a mocked event stream and asserts that prices change within a set time would stop this regression from recurring. The repository has never measured tick-to-browser latency, so instrumentation should come first, with a target of **p95 tick-to-screen under one second**. For comparison, Trade Ideas refreshes Top Lists every 15 seconds, and TrendSpider's scans refresh only on demand.

## NO TRADE is usually the honest answer, told in 18 dialects

**The second complaint has a different root cause from the first.** The platform has no clear BUY or SELL because it was never designed to issue a per-stock verdict. Its stock "signals" are next-open orders from model portfolios: BUY means enter, and SELL means exit a model holding, never short (`web/ui/src/pages/Signals.tsx:238-242`). On most days those orders barely exist. The live scan of 11 November 2026 ran 14 strategies, of which 5 were working, 2 mixed, 5 "too new" and 2 not working. The 12 non-comparison strategies produced **zero orders**; the only order was one BUY from `golden_cross`, a not-working strategy, so it was collapsed as "comparison-only" (`data/results/latest.json`, per the repository investigation).

**The one explicit verdict, the options call, is designed to say no.** Its docstring calls NO TRADE "the normal answer" (`scanner/option_verdict.py:14-17`). A BUY CALL or BUY PUT needs a tested direction, an option whose history-based fair value beats the ask plus 3% costs by at least 10%, at least a 35% chance of finishing above breakeven, a spread of 10% or less, open interest of at least 200, two or more days to expiry, and a fresh chain.

The direction gate is where most calls die. On an outlook study of 885,327 events, only about 18% of setups give a usable direction at the 5-day horizon nearest weekly expiry. Within that, **1.3% are bullish** and 16.4% bearish (`outlook_study.json`, per the investigator's approximate computation). A weekly BUY CALL is therefore almost impossible, and all six journalled verdicts were NO TRADE (`data/results/option_verdicts.json`). The intraday study rates 12 of 12 strategies "not working", 10 of them with significant losses after costs, and the pattern study finds no edge in any of 23 patterns (`intraday.json`; `pattern_study.json`).

The scarcity of BUYs is mostly the evidence speaking, and it fits SEBI's finding that 87.7–91% of individual F&O traders lose money. A platform that issued daily BUY and SELL calls from these inputs would be manufacturing them.

**Presentation is what turns honest scarcity into confusion.** The repository investigation counted **18 label vocabularies**. "Strong" means three things (an evidence grade, a strength grade and a "Strong momentum" chip), "Mixed" is both a market regime and a strategy status, and "Live" carries at least six meanings. The Chart page, where the owner actually looks, shows a "Bullish" regime pill, Outlook prose saying "no different from the average stock", a Bullish/Bearish/Neutral signals list and an options "NO TRADE" all at once.

There are also outright logic faults. A strategy SELL, which only exits a long, counts as a bearish vote in the options direction gate (`scanner/option_verdict.py:99-107`; `web/api/services/outlook_svc.py:229-232`). F&O build-ups are labelled "positioning, never a signal" yet are mapped to bullish or bearish in the outlook (`web/api/services/outlook_svc.py:264-270`). A BUY from a "too new" strategy can score 75 and display as "Strong" (`web/api/services/signal_quality.py:43-59`). And green ▲ and red ▼ candle arrows sit on the chart even though their own tests say "no edge" (`web/ui/src/pages/Chart.tsx:272-277`).

**The fix gives every symbol one answer, sourced only from tested rules.** Each symbol gets an Action card with exactly one of four states:

| State | When it shows | What the card adds |
|---|---|---|
| **BUY** | A strategy with Working status issued a next-open entry for the symbol in the latest completed-day scan, and no working strategy is exiting it | The strategy's after-cost, after-tax backtest CAGR with its timing range, the live record ("7 closed, making money"), position size and stop. Entries from mixed or too-new strategies appear only as a grey "Unproven buy" line. Not-working strategies never appear |
| **SELL (exit)** | The owner holds the stock and a working strategy's exit fires, or the owner's stop-loss is hit (`web/api/services/holdings_svc.py:60-61`) | Which rule fired and the exit price reference |
| **HOLD** | The owner holds the stock and no exit has fired | Stop distance and the next review date |
| **NO EDGE** | Anything else, replacing NO TRADE | The closest tested trigger for each working strategy, for example "momentum rank 34; enters at top 20" or "2 of 3 entry conditions met; next rebalance 1 Dec", plus the Outlook base rate |

Two edge cases follow from these rules. A model exit on a stock the owner does not hold is irrelevant to them and shows as NO EDGE. A model buy on a stock the owner already holds shows as HOLD.

Risk facts move into separate chips that never vote: the F&O ban list, results within a few days, pledge increases, and the regime filter being on. The options card keeps BUY CALL and BUY PUT but renames NO TRADE to "No options edge" and names the first check that failed. Direction is usually that check, and naming it ends the impression of arbitrariness.

A "Today" page lists every BUY and SELL across holdings and strategies. When there are none, it says so, together with how many tested actions the past 30 sessions produced. The investigator did not count zero-order days, so that figure should be measured, not assumed.

**There are honest ways to produce more BUYs, and each one is a test, not a threshold cut.** The first is late entry: backtest a "join-anytime" variant of each working ranking strategy, and if buying a top-ranked stock mid-cycle keeps its edge after costs, a model-held stock can legitimately show BUY on any day rather than only on rebalance days. The second is an index-level direction study: run the outlook study on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY themselves, because today the options direction gate borrows a study measured on stocks, since no strategy trades the index (`web/api/services/outlook_svc.py:124-125`). The third is faster promotion: use walk-forward validation (B8) to promote "too new" strategies sooner when they earn it. Each new candidate must clear the existing gates before it touches the headline (t ≥ 2, at least 200 events, Working status, and then the paper journal), and Claude stays an explainer that never emits an action state.

## The disclaimer marks a legal boundary, not a formality

**The label exists for a legal reason.** Quant Desk carries "not investment advice" in its README, first-run dialog, status bar, Settings, Signals footers, and its option, AI and Telegram notes. The README states the reason: sharing signals "requires SEBI Research Analyst registration" (`README.md:11-13`; `web/ui/src/app/AppShell.tsx:198-212`).

The rule turns on consideration. SEBI's consolidated RA Regulations define a research analyst as a person who, "for consideration", provides research services. They define consideration as "any form of economic benefit including non-cash benefit… directly or indirectly… whether from client or otherwise". Research services include buy/sell/hold recommendations, price and stop-loss targets, model portfolios and "trading calls", which means intraday, ultra-short or other non-personalised recommendations. Reg 3(1) bars anyone from acting as an RA without registration, and there is no exemption for software ([SEBI consolidated RA Regulations, amended to 10 Oct 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1792127227860.pdf)).

A tool that serves only its owner never meets that definition, which is why no "personal use" exemption is needed. A law-firm reading confirms that research offered "without any direct or indirect consideration" falls outside registration ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/securities/1574012/navigating-the-updated-regulatory-framework-for-investment-advisers-and-research-analysts)). The analysis changes the moment anyone else receives the output and the owner gains anything from it, whether ads, referral income, a paid tier or data monetisation. Index-level commentary and technical analysis of sector or index supply and demand are expressly excluded from "research report" (Reg 2(1)(w)). Stock-specific Action cards are not excluded.

**Registration would also constrain the owner's own trading.** Under the 2024–2025 amendments, registration requires a graduate degree or CFA charter plus NISM certification, a bank deposit of ₹1–10 lakh ([AZB & Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/overhaul-of-the-regulatory-framework-for-investment-advisers-and-research-analysts/)), fees capped at ₹1.51 lakh a year per family, disclosure of AI use with sole responsibility for AI output (Reg 19(vii), Reg 24(7)), five-year records of every recommendation and its rationale (Reg 25), and an annual compliance audit. The binding constraint for a trader-owner is Reg 16: an RA may not trade a recommended security from 30 days before to 5 days after the report, nor trade against their own recommendation ([SEBI consolidated RA Regulations](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1792127227860.pdf)). Publishing Quant Desk's signals would therefore restrict the owner's trading in exactly those stocks. A part-time RA category exists for people with other jobs. Advertising past performance is prohibited ([Lexibox](https://www.lexibox.in/ra/master-circular-for-research-analysts-2025/)), and until PaRRVA becomes the only route after May 2028, certified performance may be shared only when a client specifically asks ([CS Kruti](https://cskruti.com/sebi-allows-ias-ras-to-share-past-performance/); [SEBI PaRRVA circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/apr-2026/1777473564120.pdf)).

**The retail algo framework separates own-account automation from automation for others.** SEBI's circular of 4 February 2025 applied to all brokers from 1 April 2026 ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html); [TaxGuru on the glide path](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-extends-algo-trading-implementation-timeline-april-2026.html)). Under it, brokers act as principals and algo providers as their agents, every API order carries an exchange algo ID, open APIs give way to a vendor-client-specific key, a whitelisted static IP and OAuth with 2FA, and black-box providers must register as RAs. NSE set the registration threshold at **10 orders per second per exchange segment**; below it, a client's own algos carry a generic ID and need no registration, and all API sessions must log out daily ([Groww](https://groww.in/blog/nse-issues-new-compliance-norms-for-retail-algo-trading); [Zerodha](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework)).

FYERS implements the framework by accepting orders only from a registered App ID on a whitelisted static IP, requiring 2FA once per trading day with refresh-token sessions gone, converting market orders to MPP, and requiring third-party platforms to be "empanelled and hosted within the broker's infrastructure" ([FYERS notice](https://fyers.in/notice-board/new-sebi-framework-for-retail-algo-trading-from-april-01-2026)). FYERS also separates "Individual apps… for your own account" from "Third-party apps… where other FYERS clients log in" ([FYERS authentication](https://fyers.in/api-docs/v3/api/authentication/)). A registered self-built algo may be shared with family (self, spouse, dependent children and dependent parents) and no one else ([TaxGuru, circular text](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-circular-safer-participation-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading.html)). Provider empanelment runs through a trading member and takes about 30 working days, and each strategy registration takes about 10 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/nse-unveils-guidelines-for-retail-algo-trading-effective-august-1-125072301458_1.html)). Sources disagree on whether family members may share a static IP: NSE summaries say yes, while FYERS guidance says each IP must be unique.

**Data licensing is the third wall.** Live FYERS data used inside the owner's own account sits within the broker's exchange licence; showing it to other users is redistribution. A secondary summary of NSE's price list effective 1 April 2026, which needs confirming with NSE Data & Analytics, gives annual fees for an open website or app of **₹29.2 lakh** for real-time snapshots, **₹1.2 lakh** for 15-minute-delayed 1-minute snapshots, **₹1 lakh** for end-of-day data, and **₹25.5 lakh plus ₹875 per user per month** for a charting application ([OneTradeJournal](https://onetradejournal.com/platforms/why-market-data-is-delayed)). The same source cites NSE policy clause 7.4, which bars providing data to virtual-trading or simulation platforms. Educational content may use only price data at least 30 days old from 1 July 2026 ([CorpLaw Updates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/sebi-30-day-lag-educational-price-data-norms-2026)), and in August 2026 SEBI publicly cautioned against "live trading strategy" sessions ([Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-sebi-influencers-trading-strategies/)).

**Enforcement shows the cost of getting this wrong.** The recurring pattern is stock-specific calls, often live, sold through courses or groups without registration. SEBI impounded ₹17.2 crore from Baap of Chart ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/sebi-bans-baap-of-chart-securities-trading-impounds-172-crore-gained-from-followers)) and about ₹53.67 crore from Asmita Patel ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/02/sebi-bans-fininfluencer-asmita-patel-5-others-from-mkt-impound-illegal-gains-of-over-rs-53-cr)), and a reported October 2026 order told Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy to refund ₹546.17 crore plus interest, with ₹11.5 crore in penalties ([Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-orders-avadhut-sathe-trading-academy-to-refund-546-crore-imposes-115-crore-penalty/82020.html)). The summary below sets out what is permitted.

| Activity | Personal use (owner's own account) | Offered to other people |
|---|---|---|
| BUY/SELL calls, targets, stop-losses | Allowed | RA registration if any direct or indirect benefit; AI disclosure; 5-year records; Reg 16 trading restriction |
| Index or sector commentary | Allowed | Excluded from "research report" (Reg 2(1)(w)) |
| Automated orders | Allowed through a FYERS Individual app: static IP, daily 2FA, ≤10 orders/sec, MPP, no AMO | Only through an exchange-empanelled provider hosted by the broker, with each strategy registered; black-box logic also needs RA registration |
| Self-built algo above 10 orders/sec | Register through the broker; family may use it | Not shareable beyond family |
| Live NSE prices on screen | Allowed within the broker licence | NSE Data & Analytics licence |
| Paper trading on live prices | Allowed | Barred for simulation platforms (clause 7.4, secondary source) |
| Performance claims | Not applicable | Banned in advertising; on-request certified figures, then PaRRVA-verified only after May 2028 |

## A four-phase build plan reaches industry grade without crossing legal lines

"Industry-ready" means two different things here, and the plan treats them separately. As a personal tool, it means professional-grade reliability, freshness, clarity and testing, which is fully achievable engineering work. As a product for others, it means RA registration, exchange empanelment for execution and an NSE data licence, which is a legal and financial project. Each phase below removes a real complaint or risk before the next adds power. The time windows are my estimates for one developer on this codebase, not figures from the research.

| Phase | Indicative window (my estimate) | What ships | Exit benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0: Trust and clarity fixes | Weeks 1–2 | An "as of HH:MM:SS / source" stamp on every price and a red "DELAYED 15 min" badge for Yahoo. The side panel switches to the live price. The last live values are kept after 15:30 until the end-of-day scan. Scan data refetches on tab focus, and a compact ticker shows below 1,024 px. A Telegram FYERS-login reminder goes out at about 08:45. Strategy exits and F&O build-ups are removed from directional votes, strength is capped for unproven strategies, no-edge candle arrows are greyed out, and the options panel names the first failed check. The SPA `%5C` path issue is fixed (`..\PR_DESCRIPTION.md:21-22`) | No page shows two different "current" prices; every price carries a time and source |
| 1: Live data and one answer per symbol | Weeks 3–8 | A quote hub relays the existing FYERS Data socket over SSE, with the live overlay on Portfolio, Overview, Screener and Paper. 1m and 5m intraday charts are backfilled from FYERS history. Watchlists drive the socket subscription. The per-symbol Action card, the "Today" page and the consolidated vocabulary ship together, with Playwright end-to-end tests. Decisions are made on FYERS Prime (₹499/month + GST, to stream 5,000 symbols) and on moving the scheduler to an always-on host with a static IP | Measured p95 tick-to-screen under 1 s; one Action state on every symbol; no conflicting labels on the Chart page |

| 3: Compliant execution in the owner's own account | Months 5–6 | A FYERS order ticket from the Action card with sizing. GTT OCO or Smart Stop-loss/Target brackets. **Execution comes after clarity.** Automating orders from a screen that still shows "Bullish", "no different from the average stock" and "NO TRADE" side by side would only automate confusion. In the meantime, FYERS Automate already provides a compliant no-code route for simple rules ([FYERS community](https://fyers.in/community/blogs-gdppin8d/post/sebi-s-new-algo-trading-rules-kick-in-on-april-1-here-is-what-changes-Yew3vdG4CgoXk1q)).

**Phase 3 must treat the morning login as part of the routine.** Under the 2026 rules, the daily 2FA login cannot be automated away. A missing login should be a visible, alerting state, never a silent fallback to stale data.

**Testing must grow with the plan.** The Python suite, with about 385 test functions and 442 reported passing (`..\PR_DESCRIPTION.md:17`), protects the engine. The UI had no equivalent, which is how the refresh behaviour drifted unnoticed. Phase 1's end-to-end tests are as much a part of "industry-ready" as the stream itself.

## Conclusion

The competitive map inverts the usual intuition. In India, the expensive unsolved problem is not charting, scanning or execution. FYERS and TradingView give most of that away or sell it cheaply, and the global leaders cannot legally or practically serve Indian residents. The unsolved problem is trust: survivorship-free tests, dated costs and tax, and complete signal logs that include losers. Quant Desk is already ahead of every product reviewed there. The right strategy is to build the live terminal only far enough to make that evidence usable in real time, not to chase MetaTrader's or NinjaTrader's breadth. The owner's "only NO TRADE" frustration measures something real: the platform's own tests find little tradable edge, consistent with SEBI's 88–91% loss rates. A good product makes that scarcity legible instead of hiding it behind manufactured calls. The count of days with zero tested actions is a statistic worth showing.

Indian regulation is converging on the discipline Quant Desk already practises: PaRRVA-only performance claims after May 2028, algo IDs and broker hosting since April 2026, and 30-day-lagged data for education. If the owner ever opens the platform to others, the journal infrastructure becomes a ready-made compliance asset. But RA registration's restriction on trading from 30 days before to 5 days after a recommendation would bind the owner's own trading in every stock the platform recommends. Commercialising Quant Desk is therefore a decision about how the owner wants to trade, not just a feature to build, and it belongs after Phase 3 rather than before Phase 1.
